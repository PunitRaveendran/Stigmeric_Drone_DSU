# Brag Plan: PROTOPLASM

## What is this app?
An autonomous, decentralized stigmergic search-and-rescue drone swarm that finds survivors in GPS-denied disaster zones using fluid viscosity pheromone fields, tri-modal neural sensor fusion (YOLOv8 + YAMNet), and NVIDIA Object-Oriented Agents (NOOA) with local Nemotron LLMs.

## The angle
In collapsed subterranean disasters where GPS, cell towers, and Wi-Fi are completely severed, you can't use cloud AI or centralized base stations. PROTOPLASM proves that 24 autonomous micro-drones communicating via digital pheromones and local NVIDIA agent debate can map unknown terrain, reject single-channel thermal/audio decoys, and execute life-saving rescues with 0ms lag.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep black Fog of War. A flashing radar ping reveals the headline:
**"WHAT IF 24 DRONES COULD RESCUE SURVIVORS WITHOUT GPS, WI-FI, OR A PILOT?"**

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Stigmergic Fluid Heatmap:** Drones launch with 360° fan-out velocity, laying down digital pheromones that diffuse across a 60 FPS visualizer.
- **NVIDIA NOOA / Nemotron Debate:** When ambiguous signals hit, drones elect a spatial lead and stream local Nemotron LLM reasoning to reject false-positive decoys (hot rubble / wind).
- **Consensus & Rescue Extraction:** Multi-angle convergence locks onto trapped survivors, triggering a +500 PTS mission score boost and dynamic attention shifting.

## Outro / punchline
Swarm reaches 100% mission completion and self-healing RTL return to base.
**"NO CENTRAL CONTROLLER. NO CLOUD DEPENDENCY. 100% AUTONOMOUS."**

## User flow worth showing
1. **Launch & Exploration:** 24 drones fan out into dark Fog of War, streaming live telemetry and altitude rings (15m, 25m, 35m).
2. **Ambiguity Resolution:** NOOA Negotiator evaluates multi-modal sensor signals (YOLO Camera + YAMNet Audio) from multiple approach angles.
3. **Extraction & Attention Shift:** Survivor extracted, pheromone peak dissolves, and the swarm scatters to map remaining disaster sectors.
